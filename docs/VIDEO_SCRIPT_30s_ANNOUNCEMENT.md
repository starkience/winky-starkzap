# Winky Starkzap — 30s Announcement Video Script

**Tone:** Dynamic, emotional, hyped, over-dramatic  
**Duration:** ~30 seconds  
**Pacing:** Fast cuts, punchy lines, dramatic pauses

---

## SCRIPT

**[0:00 – 0:02]**  
*Black or slow-mo: ancient / evolutionary imagery — eyes, light, dust.*

**VO (slow, epic, documentary):**  
*"For millions of years… humans blinked."*

**[0:02 – 0:05]**  
*Extreme close-up: eye. Single blink in slow motion. Soft, organic.*

**VO (measured, each word weighted):**  
*"Each blink… slowly lubricated… cleaned… and protected their eyes."*

**[0:05 – 0:06]**  
*Beat. Black or hard cut.*

**VO (shift — grave, then rising):**  
*"Until today."*

**[0:06 – 0:09]**  
*Cut to product. Logo or app UI. Music kicks in.*

**VO (announcer, hyped):**  
*"Introducing Wink. Allowing humans to earn while blinking."*

**[0:09 – 0:12]**  
*Rapid cuts: webcam, blinks, counter, Starknet-style visuals.*

**VO (full energy):**  
*"No wallet. No gas. No seed phrases. Every blink — a real transaction on Starknet. Zero cost. You blink, we put it on-chain."*

**[0:13 – 0:16]**  
*Montage: people blinking, counter climbing, “gasless” / “on-chain” text.*

**VO (over-dramatic, triumphant):**  
*"The future isn’t in your wallet. It’s in the one thing you do twenty thousand times a day."*

**[0:17]**  
*Pause. One slow blink into camera.*

**VO (quiet, then spike):**  
*"One blink. One transaction. Forever."*

**[0:18 – 0:22]**  
*Logo lock. Tagline on screen.*

**VO (big finish):**  
*"Winky Starkzap. Blink. On-chain."*

**[0:23 – 0:30]**  
*End card: app name, “Blink. On-chain.”, CTA (e.g. Try it now / starkzap.io). Hold 5–7 seconds.*

- **0:05–0:06:** “Until today” — tension or silence before the turn.  
- **0:06–0:09:** Music or impact hits. Product reveal.  
- **0:09–0:22:** Driving, modern, “tech launch” track.

## WORD COUNT

~115 words spoken. With the slow, epic pacing of the opening (“For millions of years…” through “Until today”), total runtime stays ~30 seconds. If the read is faster, trim the middle montage or hold the end card longer.
